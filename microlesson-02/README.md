### Where Does AI Show Up in Data Work?

The modern data workflow includes many steps. Here are a few spots where AI is already making an impact:

- **Data collection:** Automating web scraping, data extraction, or ingestion.
- **Data cleaning & prep:** Detecting outliers, filling in missing values, suggesting corrections.
- **Analysis:** Generating insights, finding patterns, or predicting outcomes.
- **Visualization & storytelling:** Summarizing findings, suggesting the best chart types, writing executive summaries.
- **Reporting & automation:** Generating dashboards, sending alerts, writing up regular reports.

> 💡 **AI can help with tasks that are repetitive, require pattern recognition, or need quick summarization. But… some things still require a human touch!**

## Types of AI in Data: Predictive, Prescriptive, Generative

Let’s clarify the main “types” of AI tools you’ll encounter in data roles:

| **Type of AI** | **What it does** | **Data Example** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Predictive AI** | Forecasts what’s likely to happen | “Which customers are likely to churn?”<br>“What will sales be next month?” |
| **Prescriptive AI** | Recommends what to do next | “What actions should we take to reduce churn?”<br>“Which product to promote for highest ROI?” |
| **Generative AI** | Creates new content or code | “Write a Python script to clean this data.”<br>“Generate a summary of these results.” |

## What AI Can—and Can’t—Do in Data

AI is powerful, but not magic. Here’s a reality check:

### **AI Can:**
- Speed up routine tasks (cleaning, basic analysis)
- Surface patterns or anomalies you might miss
- Suggest next steps or generate reports
- Automate repetitive reporting or alerting

### **AI Can’t or Shouldn’t:**
- Replace your judgment for high-stakes decisions
- Catch every nuance or context (especially with sensitive or complex data)
- Guarantee perfect accuracy (especially with new, messy, or biased data)
- Work safely without oversight—*you’re still responsible for the results!*
