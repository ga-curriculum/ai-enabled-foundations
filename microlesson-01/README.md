**Learning objective:** By the end of this microlesson, you’ll be able to describe what it means to be an AI-enabled data professional and identify areas in your workflow where AI could provide value.

---

Data teams are using AI and automation to transform how they clean, analyze, and share insights. But being "AI-enabled" isn't just about using a flashy tool—it's about:
- Understanding what AI can (and can't) do for your data work
- Spotting new opportunities to boost your own productivity and creativity
- Building the skills to stay ahead as data and AI keep evolving

---

## Warm-Up: What Does “AI-Enabled” Mean in Data?

> 💡 **Reflection:**  
> What comes to mind when you hear “AI-enabled data professional”?  
> Jot down one or two words or phrases.

**Our focus today:**  
- What does it *really* mean to be AI-enabled, AI-augmented, or even AI-superpowered as a data professional?
- Where do you see yourself on that spectrum?

---

### The AI-Enabled, Augmented, and Superpowered Data Pro

| **Enabled** | **Augmented** | **Superpowered** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Understands AI basics and can use GenAI features with confidence | Integrates AI into day-to-day data workflows, boosting speed and insight | Designs new processes that *fully embed* AI, transforming what’s possible with data |
| “I know how to use it.” | “I make my work better with it.” | “I’m inventing new ways to work.” |

> ❓**Quick check:**  
> Where would you put yourself right now—enabled, augmented, or superpowered? Why?

## Icebreaker: Try Out Generative AI for Data

Let’s break the ice and have some fun experimenting with GenAI tools. No need to be an expert!

### **Task:**
1. Open [duck.ai](https://duck.ai) in your browser. You can chat with different AI models here!
2. Choose *one* of the sample prompts below—or make up your own!
3. Copy and paste a small dataset (or describe one), and try asking:
    - **Draw a picture using text, markdown, or code.**
    - **What does it mean to be an AI-powered HR professional?**
    - **Let’s play 20 questions!**
    - **If you could be any human, who would you be and why?**

## 🤔 Think about it: Where Do You Want AI in Your Data Work?

Take a minute to think about your current workflow as a data professional. Consider the following:

- What parts of your data work do you want to **own yourself** (always human)?
- What parts would you be open to **collaborating on with AI**?
- What parts might you want to **fully automate**?

**Jot down your answers. Then, if in a live session, pair up or join a small group to discuss.**
